Good catch, and it was a real design flaw rather than a stray file.

**What was there:** the `.published` marker — 1.8KB of spent receipt that my publisher renamed rather than removed. I'd chosen renaming during development so nothing could be lost irrecoverably, but the effect was litter accumulating in the one place you actually read. An inbox that fills with its own spent paperwork stops being an inbox.

**Fixed properly rather than just deleted.** The receipt is audit material, so it now moves to `<jobdir>/receipt.md` on publish, sitting alongside the payload, extract, audit and inventory. If no job directory exists it still falls back to the rename rather than deleting something unrecoverable.

**Your inbox is back to your five TODOs.**

Worth noting this would have compounded: every published job would have left one behind, so you'd have found it eventually with a dozen of them. Better caught on the first real run.

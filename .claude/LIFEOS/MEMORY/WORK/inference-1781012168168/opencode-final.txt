What decision are we working on?

Useful starting points:

1. Current role, comp, equity/bonus, location, and seniority.
2. Target outcome: raise, promotion, move, partner track, exit, fractional/advisory, or negotiation.
3. Constraints: visa, notice period, family, location, risk appetite.
4. Timeline: immediate negotiation, 3-month move, 12-month career repositioning.
5. Any offer, review cycle, or recruiter conversation already in play.

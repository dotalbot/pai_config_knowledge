# Strategies

> How you approach your missions, goals, and problems — the playbook.

- **S0:** Learn by consuming broadly first — read, watch, get the ebooks — then lock it in through thought experiments and prototyping.
- **S1:** Always prototype before forming an opinion. Hands-on beats theory every time. No commitment without a working test.
- **S2:** Build something and show it working — demonstration over explanation. Share everything with like-minded people; knowledge compounds in community.
  **Status 2026-08-24: HALF LIVE.** The building half runs constantly. The
  *sharing* half is aspiration — a lot has been built since June and none of it
  has left the machine. Not a flaw in the strategy: until now there was no
  obvious audience. The new organisation changes that, and the potential to
  connect with like-minded people is the thing that has actually shifted.
  **Watch item, not an action:** if S2 is still half-live at the next review,
  the constraint is not audience.
- **S3:** Work with people who care about the same things — find the practitioners, avoid the evangelists.

## Notes

Strategies are the *how* that connects goals to missions. A strategy is durable across specific goals — if you changed all your goals, your strategies might still apply.

*The DA uses strategies to suggest *methods* aligned with how you actually operate, not generic best practices that don't match your style.*

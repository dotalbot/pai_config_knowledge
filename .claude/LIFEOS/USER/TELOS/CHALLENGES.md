# Challenges

> Personal patterns, friction points, and persistent failure modes you're aware of.

- **C0:** Focus and distraction — ADHD means shiny new things constantly compete with what I'm supposed to be doing.
  **Named honestly 2026-08-24: LifeOS itself is sometimes the shiny thing.**
  Dom said so directly when asked. But the intent is deliberate, not drift — he
  is driving LifeOS as the cornerstone for how he keeps things together and gets
  results, and is determined to make it how he goes about his day.
  **What this means for me:** building LifeOS is not automatically progress.
  The test is whether a piece of it gets *used* afterwards. The Conveyor queue
  earns its place the first time a real transcript goes through it; until then
  it is potential. When Dom is deep in the system rather than deep in the work,
  the useful question is "is this the cornerstone, or is this the shiny thing
  today?" — asked once, not nagged.
- **C1:** Staying organised and retaining what I learn — knowledge slips without a system to catch it.
  **Status 2026-08-24: actively improving.** The vault discipline (Sources vs
  Thinking), the Cortex memory layer and the Hindsight recall layer all exist
  now and did not in June.
- **C2:** Too many ideas, not enough system — projects stall, context gets lost, and switching between them is painful. Need infrastructure that keeps things moving without me having to rebuild context from scratch every time.
  **Status 2026-08-24: this is exactly what G2 is building**, and it is the live
  goal. C2 is the challenge; G2 is the response to it.

## Notes

Challenges are different from problems-you're-solving (see PROBLEMS.md). Challenges are *about you* — procrastination patterns, energy management, emotional triggers, ways you sabotage yourself.

Naming them here gives the DA context to coach against them instead of accidentally feeding them.

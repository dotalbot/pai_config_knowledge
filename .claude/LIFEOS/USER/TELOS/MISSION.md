# Mission

> What you're here to do — the most durable north star.

- **M0:** Deliver real, measurable AI value to business — working products, not hype.
- **M1:** Master hands-on AI application as a practitioner, not just an architect — get technical and stay there.
- **M2:** Bridge enterprise architecture judgment with deep engineering execution to make AI actually land in organisations.

## Notes

Missions are more stable than goals. A goal is done when its ISCs pass; a mission keeps going. If your mission list shifts every few months, you're describing goals.

Good missions answer: *if the world had more of X because of you, what would X be?*

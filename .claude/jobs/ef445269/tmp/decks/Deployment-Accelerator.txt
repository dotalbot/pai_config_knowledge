=== SLIDE 3 ===
Document purpose
Purpose
The purpose of this document is to provide an overview and definition of the Frontier Accelerate for Copilot Program and details on the post-sales engagement:
M365 Copilot Deployment Accelerator
This delivery guide outlines a flexible engagement model. For each step, the guide provides a delivery structure, engagement delivery examples, and discusses necessary activities, assets and resources you may need to familiarize yourself for this engagement.
Audience
This document is intended to be used by partner sellers, practice leads, project managers, field consultants, and other partner resources with competencies in Microsoft 365 Copilot.
Assumptions
Partner resources involved in the delivery of these engagements have read and understand the content of this delivery guide.
Partner understands that this document is not intended to be shared directly with customers.
Other notes
If you believe you have found an error or have a suggestion for improving this document, please complete this form.

Frontier Accelerate for Copilot
New Frontier Accelerate post-sales engagement for FY27!
As AI technology captures global attention, organizations have a unique opportunity to accelerate business value by leveraging AI. M365 Copilot Deployment Accelerator objectives and outcomes
DELIVERING PARTNER IMPACT
The following represents possible objectives and potential outcomes for delivering this engagement. You may choose to focus efforts in one or more areas based on your customer’s needs.
Objectives
Assess customer readiness for a secure and compliant M365 Copilot deployment.
Remediate technical, security, and governance concerns.
Rapidly activate Microsoft 365 Copilot scenarios focusing on high-impact, high-frequency workflows.
Engage and train Champions on the high-value scenarios that transform business processes.
Target outcomes

Activate Champions community to expand usage and share impactful best practices.
Continue an organizational AI-First transformation journey reimagining how work gets done.

=== SLIDE 14 ===
Engagement approach
DELIVERING PARTNER IMPACT
This engagement is flexible and modular. Partners can choose to deliver any combination of activities and utilize any set of resources and assets provided in this guide or use their own assets. This may be based on partner preference, customer requirements or other considerations.
Please see the Frontier Accelerate for Copilot Program Guide and MCI Incentive Guide for minimum delivery hours required for funding eligibility.
A reminder: Microsoft’s funding is a co-investment to support M365 Copilot Deployment Accelerator deliveries and is not intended to cover the cost of all the deployment, implementation or adoption work.

=== SLIDE 22 ===
Partner pre-engagement checklist
Preparing for the Engagement
Get ready for delivering the M365 Copilot Deployment Accelerator by reviewing the delivery guide, identifying assets and resources that you will use, aligning with your customer stakeholders to agree on the scope of the engagement.
Make sure to review the delivery guide and engagement content:
Keep in mind this is a flexible engagement.
Identify activity categories and corresponding activities that you will deliver to the customer. Please remember you are not required to deliver activities in any particular order.
Select and download identified assets and resources. Review them and get ready for the delivery.
Schedule and facilitate the pre-engagement planning meeting. Use this deck during the meeting.
In the meeting, be sure to:
Align and confirm customer stakeholders (executive sponsor, communications lead, training leads, department leads, IT stakeholders)
If any high-value scenarios were evaluated during the Envisioning engagement, please review them here and adopt if needed.
Define success of the engagement by documenting vision and goals (Review this resource prior to the meeting: Identify business goals and success metrics)
Share Create an AI Council guide and The Strategic CIOs Generative AI Playbook with your customer.
If necessary, provide Customer Pre-Engagement Questionnaire
Identify Champions, Community Leads and user groups.
Agree on daily/weekly project calls with customer to keep track of the engagement progress and feedback.
Agree on the engagement scope, deliverables and the start date of the engagement.
Request trials, if necessary

=== SLIDE 36 ===
Activity category definitions
Engagement Delivery Structure
Review the seven activity categories and their definitions to understand how we have defined them.
Activity category
Activity category definition
Scenario Discovery & Solution Design
This category includes activities related to the process of identifying and prioritizing high-value business scenarios that can unlock further potential with Microsoft 365 Copilot.
Business Value Analysis, Measurement, & Reporting
This category includes activities across the end-to-end value stream, such as defining methods to qualify and quantify value, setting business objectives, establishing value metrics, measuring cost and value, and realizing value outcomes.
Showcase AI in Action
This category includes art of the possible experiences showcasing Frontier transformation demos, stories, product pitch decks.
Security, Compliance & Governance
This category includes activities related to the delivery of information, planning, configuration, development, setup, and technical deployment activities in support of security, compliance, and governance frameworks, technologies, reporting and operations.
Agents, Solutions & Initiatives Development
This category includes activities related to the technical design, development, testing, and deployment of a service, agent, application, automation, or non-technical initiative designed to deliver business value.
Administration & Operations
This category includes activities related to the administration, operations, setup, configuration, monitoring, and maintenance of a platform, a service, cloud infrastructure, platform and application features and other elements which require ongoing administration.
Training & Adoption
This category includes activities related to the transfer of knowledge, learnings, skills, and capabilities to Champions, end users, administrators, and other stakeholders – ultimately driving greater adoption and utilization of a platform, service, solution, or technical capability.

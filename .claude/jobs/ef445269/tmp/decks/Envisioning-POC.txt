=== SLIDE 3 ===
Document purpose
Purpose
This guide defines the new Frontier Accelerate for Copilot: Envisioning & Proof of Concept (POC) engagement.
This is a flexible pre-sales motion that helps customers move beyond AI features toward Frontier Transformation: re-inventing end-to-end business workflows with Microsoft 365 Copilot, agents, and Agent 365. The guide is structured around identifying high-value scenarios, building a defensible business case with measurable ROI, and proving value through a focused POC
Audience
This document is intended to be used by partner sellers, practice leads, project managers, field consultants, and other partner resources with competencies in Microsoft 365 Copilot.
Assumptions
Partner resources involved in the delivery of this engagement have read and understand the content of this delivery guide.
Partner understands that this document is not intended to be shared directly with customers.
Other notes
If you believe you have found an error or correction needed in this document, please complete this form.

=== SLIDE 19 ===
When to use Agent 365 Demo Led PoC
Important: Before starting an Agent 365 customer conversation, use the guidance below to decide whether to start with Agent 365 customer pitch deck, or progress into the Agent 365 demo-led PoC. Run agent discovery first, where appropriate, to understand existing agent usage and identify the most relevant customer scenarios.
Customer Pitch Deck
Use Agent 365 Customer Pitch Deck when:
Introductory customer conversations
Executive briefings and awareness sessions
High-level capability demonstrations
Early-stage opportunity qualification
Focus on vision and value
Security/compliance teams are not yet engaged
There are not enough agents live in the environment
Agent 365 demo-led PoC
Use the Agent 365 demo-led PoC when:
The customer has clear interest in agent governance, security, or operational readiness
Discovery has identified agent risks, priorities, or stakeholder questions to explore
The customer wants to validate scenarios across Observe, Govern, and Secure
Multiple stakeholders need a structured engagement to align on requirements and outcomes
The engagement needs to produce recommendations, priorities, and next steps
"Tell me about Agent 365."
"Help me understand how Agent 365 applies to my environment and use cases."

The customer has committed to a Copilot-credit budget, agreed to activate M365 Copilot licenses (trials available for M365 Copilot SKU) and purchased Copilot credits for this engagement
Business and IT sponsors are aligned on a pilot cohort (start with a small size)
Success metrics of the engagement identified, and cost baseline can be captured
You are proving the value of Copilot Cowork live in the customer’s environment with the identified pilot cohort

=== SLIDE 23 ===
Engagement flow: the four engagement pillars
Frontier accelerate for copilot: Envisioning & POC
The Frontier Accelerate for Copilot: Envisioning & POC engagement can be organized into four simple pillars to help you meet the customer where they are and guide envisioning and/or proof of concept conversations. You do not need to deliver activities in a particular order, but the recommended flow will help with building on prior conversations and achieve a successful engagement.
Assess
Assess customer’s needs and readiness for AI enabled transformation.
Gain an understanding of the customers current state position in terms of strategy, vision, technical, organizational, and business readiness.
Facilitate executive-level interviews, data analysis and workshopping to identify high-value scenarios across priority departments.
Inspire
Showcase the art of the possible through transformation stories, demos, and customer case studies.
Position Copilot and agents as AI teammates grounded in the customer’s data.
Establish why this matters: business value, process reinvention, and the role of governance and adoption.
Design
Develop a preliminary business case or value realization plan for the customer.
Define any relevant technical or functional requirements and design needed for a POC.
Build an adoption strategy as necessary.
Define next steps and timeline to develop and implement the solution.
Proof of Concept
Deliver a proof of concept in the customer’s environment, demonstrating real value using their data and scenarios and enabling the customer to validate feasibility and make implementation decisions.

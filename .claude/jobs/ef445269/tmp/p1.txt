
STRICT BRAND SPECIFICATION — The Inform Team (theinformteam.com). Follow exactly.
TYPEFACE: Montserrat ONLY, for every single word. Headline weight 400 (Light/Regular — NOT bold, the brand does not shout). Sub-headings weight 600. Body weight 400. Body text has slight letter-spacing.
COLOURS — use ONLY these hex values:
  #001450 deep navy — the dominant ground
  #FFFFFF white
  #F2F2F2 light warm grey — page background
  #FF275B magenta-pink — ACCENT ONLY, used sparingly for emphasis, thin rules, one key highlight. NEVER a large fill.
  #333333 dark grey for body text on light
  #7FEBE9 mint and #A08DF4 lilac — permitted ONLY as small differentiators (a thin bar, a small icon), never as large areas.
RESTRAINT — this is critical: clean, crisp, corporate, generous whitespace. NO gradients. NO drop shadows. NO 3D or isometric effects. NO glow. NO photographic textures. NO clip-art people. Flat vector only. Thin line weights. The composition must feel calm and premium, like a professional consultancy website. Do not overwhelm with colour — most of the canvas is navy or white.
OUTPUT: a website hero infographic, landscape 16:9, high resolution, crisp vector-style edges, text perfectly legible and correctly spelled.
